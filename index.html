Tourism Project

Explore Bandung's hiking trails with clearer travel information.

Bandung Hiking Trails Explorer is a simple web project that helps users discover hiking trails in Bandung and surrounding areas. The website introduces useful trail information in a clean layout so visitors can understand each route before planning a trip.

What this product helps users do

Users can compare trail details, estimate hiking time, and check route accessibility from nearby stations or public transport stops.

Key Information

Important details shown by the product

Trail Difficulty

Each route can be grouped into beginner, moderate, or more challenging hikes to help users choose the best match for their experience.

Duration

Estimated hiking duration gives visitors a quick idea of how much time they should prepare for the trip.

Elevation Gain

Elevation gain helps users understand how steep the trail may feel and how much physical effort might be needed.

Accessibility

Nearby stations and transport stops make it easier to see how visitors can reach the starting point of each hiking trail.

Project Overview

Why this website matters

For Travelers

The website gives a simple introduction for people who want to enjoy outdoor tourism around Bandung without reading complex route descriptions.

For Planning

Visitors can review trail characteristics first, then decide which route is suitable for a short morning hike or a longer weekend adventure.
